Our Mission

Sharing Kashmir's Heart, One Journey at a Time

Travel With Yousuf was born from a simple desire: to share the unparalleled beauty and warm hospitality of Kashmir with the world. We are not just a travel company; we are storytellers, locals, and passionate hosts dedicated to creating authentic experiences that go beyond the typical tourist trail.

We believe that travel should be transformative. It should connect you to the place, its people, and its culture. That’s why every itinerary we design is handcrafted with care, ensuring you feel the magic of the valley, worry-free.

The Man Behind the Magic

Meet Mohammad Yousuf

Founder & Managing Director

"For over 18 years, I have had the privilege of welcoming travelers to my home, Kashmir. My vision has always been to offer more than just a vacation. I want to offer a connection—a chance to experience the genuine warmth of our culture, the taste of our food, and the breathtaking beauty of our landscapes as if you were family."

"At Travel With Yousuf, we handle every detail with personal care, from finding the perfect houseboat to planning a route that captures the season's best. Your comfort and joy are our highest priorities. We invite you to come as a traveler and leave with Kashmir in your heart."
